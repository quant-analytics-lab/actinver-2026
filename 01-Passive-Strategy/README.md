# Passive Investment Strategy | Actinver 2026

## 1. Project Overview

This project documents the development and performance evaluation of a passive investment portfolio as part of the **Actinver Investment Challenge 2026**.

The strategy uses Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) to obtain diversified exposure to the U.S. stock market, the Mexican equity market, and international developed markets.

Rather than attempting to predict individual stock prices, the portfolio follows a predefined asset allocation and evaluates its performance over time.

**Strategy Start Date:** October 10, 2026  
**Initial Virtual Capital:** MXN $1,000,000  
**Investment Approach:** Passive ETF Portfolio  
**Primary Objective:** Long-term capital growth through diversified market exposure.

## 3. Investment Methodology

The strategy follows five principles:

1. **Diversification:** Invest across multiple equity indices and geographic markets.
2. **Strategic Allocation:** Establish target portfolio weights before investing.
3. **Disciplined Investing:** Avoid making allocation decisions based exclusively on short-term market movements.
4. **Portfolio Monitoring:** Evaluate returns, volatility, and changes in asset allocation.
5. **Rebalancing:** Review portfolio weights periodically and adjust them when appropriate.

## 4. Performance Evaluation

The portfolio will be evaluated using the following financial metrics:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Cumulative Return | Measure total portfolio growth or loss |
| Annualized Volatility | Estimate investment risk |
| Sharpe Ratio | Evaluate risk-adjusted performance |
| Maximum Drawdown | Measure the largest decline from a previous portfolio peak |
| Benchmark Comparison | Compare portfolio performance against relevant market indices |
| Portfolio Allocation Drift | Identify deviations from target ETF weights |

Performance will be calculated using portfolio market values and documented transactions.

## 6. Research Questions

This project aims to investigate:

- Can a diversified ETF portfolio generate competitive risk-adjusted returns?
- How does the portfolio perform compared with its individual market benchmarks?
- How much does the portfolio benefit from geographic diversification?
- How does a small portfolio with weekly contributions compare with a larger virtual portfolio?
- What is the impact of rebalancing and portfolio concentration on overall performance?
