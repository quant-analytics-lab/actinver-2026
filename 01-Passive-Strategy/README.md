# Passive Investment Strategy | Actinver 2026

## 1. Project Overview

This project documents the implementation and performance evaluation of a passive ETF investment strategy developed for the **Actinver Investment Challenge 2026**.

The portfolio is designed to provide exposure to U.S. equities, the Mexican stock market, international developed markets, and the technology sector.

The strategy follows a predefined asset allocation rather than attempting to predict individual stock prices. Its performance will be monitored using quantitative financial indicators and compared against relevant market benchmarks.

### Diversification Considerations

Each ETF serves a different role within the portfolio:

- **VOO:** Broad exposure to large U.S. companies.
- **QQQ:** Exposure to the Nasdaq-100, including major technology-oriented companies.
- **DIA:** Exposure to established U.S. companies represented by the Dow Jones Industrial Average.
- **NAFTRAC:** Exposure to the Mexican equity market.
- **VEA:** Exposure to developed international markets outside the United States.
- **VGT:** Dedicated exposure to the U.S. information technology sector.

Although the portfolio includes six ETFs, their underlying holdings are not completely independent.

In particular, VOO, QQQ, DIA, and VGT may share exposure to large U.S. companies. Therefore, the portfolio may experience concentration risk despite holding multiple ETFs.

## 3. Investment Methodology

The strategy follows five principles:

1. **Diversification:** Allocate capital across different equity indices, geographic markets, and sectors.
2. **Strategic Allocation:** Define portfolio weights before investing and document actual allocations.
3. **Disciplined Investing:** Avoid changing the strategy exclusively in response to short-term market movements.
4. **Portfolio Monitoring:** Evaluate returns, volatility, concentration, and allocation changes.
5. **Rebalancing:** Review portfolio weights periodically and adjust them when necessary.

### Portfolio Construction

The initial virtual capital was MXN $1,000,000.

## 4. Performance Evaluation

The portfolio will be evaluated using the following financial metrics:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Cumulative Return | Measure total portfolio growth or loss |
| Annualized Volatility | Estimate variability in portfolio returns |
| Sharpe Ratio | Evaluate risk-adjusted performance |
| Sortino Ratio | Measure performance relative to downside risk |
| Maximum Drawdown | Identify the largest decline from a previous portfolio peak |
| Benchmark Comparison | Compare performance against relevant market indices |
| Portfolio Allocation Drift | Identify deviations from target weights |
| Asset Contribution | Measure each ETF's contribution to portfolio performance |

Performance will be calculated using historical market data, confirmed transactions, and portfolio valuations.

Changes caused by additional contributions or withdrawals will be distinguished from investment returns.

## 6. Research Questions

This project aims to investigate:

- Can a diversified ETF portfolio generate competitive risk-adjusted returns?
- How does the portfolio perform compared with relevant market benchmarks?
- How much geographic and sector diversification does the portfolio actually achieve?
- What is the impact of technology sector exposure on overall performance?

- How do allocation drift and rebalancing affect portfolio risk and returns?
